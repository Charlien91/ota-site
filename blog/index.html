Overtime Analytix

Sensor-instrumented sports equipment: the OTX Football that measures throws from inside the ball, and the OTX smartwatch with the SwingIQ swing analyzer for golf, baseball and tennis.

Overtime Analytix builds predictive models and puts real sensors inside real sports equipment. The OTX Football is a 3D-printed airless football that measures spiral quality, spin rate and hang time from inside the ball. The OTX smartwatch runs SwingIQ, a golf, baseball and tennis swing analyzer that reports club speed, tempo and attack angle on your wrist. Hardware and firmware are designed and assembled in the USA.
